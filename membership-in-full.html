Program descriptions

Community Stewards and Founding Members can join as few or as many of the membership benefits as they desire. We ask that you accurately RSVP and attend every session you commit to join.

Education about money

  • The Invest for Better course. A cohort of about 20 women, for six weeks in a row will meet to discuss and define your values and learn the investment tools. Banking, stocks and bonds, and private investing. Developed by Janine Firpo, led by impact investment advisor Lauren Sercu, and facilitated by Tori Horton.
  • Finance workshops. Four times a year we gather in person to hear from national money experts. Evening gatherings, with leaders our community selects, on everything from your personal money story to specific investment strategies. Included for Founding Members and Community Stewards. Associate Members are welcome at $150 per workshop.
  • Office hours. Monthly from September to May, a community hour on video with Tori and other members. Bring your own beverage of choice and let's get real about the topics that matter to you.

Access to deals and due diligence

  • Four pitch gatherings. Founders pitch live on a video call. Every paying member can join and ask questions.
  • The data room. Review the financials, the business plan, and the marketing strategy for yourself.
  • The research. Every deal carries roughly 40 hours of research before anyone writes a check. Together we size the market, evaluate the opportunity, and review the terms.
  • Your own investment decision. You invest deal by deal, and money moves only when you say yes.

A kind and effective community

Small dollar investing only works when we do it together. We show up with capital, with knowledge, with relationships, with visibility, and with advocacy. We lead with curiosity and assume positive intent. We are kind and we are effective, both, always. We amplify each other's voices and operate from abundance.

The second family seat. Every paying membership can add a second seat for your mother or your daughter. It is $500 a year for Founding Members, and included for Community Stewards. She gets a full membership and all the benefits. She completes her own application and consents for herself. We want talking about money with family to be a normal conversation.

The calendar

More than 20 gatherings a year. We gather September through May and rest in the summer. In-person gatherings happen along the Wasatch Front, and pitch meetings are online. You get every event date the day you join, and if you join mid-season you get access to everything available online.

The annual retreat

One weekend a year, together. The retreat is a ticketed event, and every member is offered the member rate, at every tier, including Associate. Community Stewards attend as part of their membership at no additional cost.

The monthly member update

Written and sent by Tori, monthly through the season. It carries the month's gatherings and what is ahead, class and workshop notes, member and founder spotlights, and progress toward the annual report. A member who chooses to watch rather than write checks stays fully in the loop.

The annual report

Published every year: what we paid to bring in financial experts, what capital reached women-founded companies, how many women learned to invest or wrote a first check, and what we gave back to Utah's women founders and their community. The first gift is set: $2,500 to Mama High-Five Grants in 2026. Community Stewards are named in it.

If you want to invest

Membership and investing run side by side and never gate each other. A member who never writes a check is a full member in good standing, forever.

Four things are required before anyone can invest in a deal:

  • Accredited investor status under the SEC definition: income over $200,000 a year, or $300,000 with a spouse, for the past two years; or net worth over $1 million, leaving out your primary home.
  • A substantive relationship established beforehand: an intake form, a private conversation, and time.
  • A deal-by-deal yes. There is no standing commitment. Each deal is a separate decision with its own minimum.
  • Clean rails. Investment money flows through the deal's own account. Dues and investment dollars never mix.
Membership provides access to education, networking, and community benefits. Membership does not guarantee access to private investment opportunities. Membership dues are not an investment. Startups are risky and you can lose money you invest.
5

Questions

Do I have to invest?

No. Membership offers education, training, and a community. If you want to invest, you decide deal by deal and make your own money decisions.

What does my membership pricing actually pay for?

Managing communities, hosting gatherings, and finding deals takes a lot of time and work. We value cognitive labor and compensate our team so this community stays sustainable beyond any one person's leadership. Your dues pay the teachers, the experts, and the work of bringing us together.

I have never invested in my life. Will I be lost?

The classes are built for exactly where you are. We learn finance terms and talk about money in plain English, starting from the beginning. Most members are learning alongside you. That is the point of the community.

What is an accredited investor, and do I need to be one?

You do not need to be an accredited investor to join. Every member can watch founder pitch meetings and read deal memos, which is a great way to learn. You do have to certify that you are accredited before you are eligible to invest in one of our deals.

Could I lose money?

Yes. Startups are risky and you can lose money you invest. This is why we research every deal, members spread their own checks, and no one is ever required to invest. Membership dues are not an investment; they purchase a year of classes and community gatherings.

When and where do you meet?

We gather September through May and rest in the summer. In-person gatherings happen along the Wasatch Front, and the pitch meetings are online. Calendar events are published in advance.

How does joining work?

Applying is free. Tell us about yourself, book a conversation with Tori, and decide together whether membership is right for you. Nothing is final before that conversation.

Who can hold my second seat?

Your mother or your daughters are invited, because we want more families learning to talk with each other about money. The second seat is $500 a year for Founding Members, and included for Community Stewards. She gets a full membership and all the benefits. She will complete her own application and consent for herself.
