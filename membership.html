Membership Benefits

Pick your own adventure.

Opt in for as few or as many of these resources as you want.

1

Education about money

Six weeks of training, plus workshops and office hours
  • Invest for Better Course: Join a cohort of about 20 women for a 6-week money program where you define your values and learn about investment tools. We explore banking, stocks and bonds, and private investing. Developed by Janine Firpo, this course is led by impact investment advisor Lauren Sercu and facilitated by Tori Horton.
  • Finance Workshops: Four times a year we gather in person to hear from national money experts. These evening gatherings host leaders our community selects, with topics ranging from understanding your personal money story to diving deep on investment strategies.
  • Office Hour Invite: Monthly from September to May, join a community hour to hang with Tori and other members and talk about money on a video call. Bring your own beverage of choice!

Total value estimate: Other programs charge $2,000 a year for classes like these. The classes alone are worth more than the membership price.

2

Access to deals and due diligence

Learn by doing, and participate in Utah deals
  • Attend 4 pitch gatherings: Founders pitch live on a video call, and every paying member can join and ask questions.
  • See the Data Room: Get access to review the financials, the business plan, and the marketing strategy.
  • Participate in the research: Every deal carries roughly 40 hours of research before anyone writes a check. Together, we size the market, evaluate the opportunity, and review the deal terms.
  • Own your investment: You invest deal by deal, and money moves only when you say yes.

If you've been thinking about starting or joining a fund, this membership experience is designed for you.

Questions about Membership

Do I have to invest?
No. Membership offers education, training, and a community. If you want to invest, you decide deal by deal, and make your own money decisions.
What does my membership pricing actually pay for?
Managing communities, hosting gatherings, and finding deals takes a lot of time and work. We value cognitive labor and compensate our team members so that this community can remain sustainable beyond any one person's leadership. Your dues pay the teachers, the experts, and the work of bringing us together.
I've never invested in my life. Will I be lost?
The classes are built for exactly where you are. We learn finance terms and talk about money in plain English, starting from the beginning. Most members are learning alongside you; that is the point of the community.
What is an "accredited investor," and do I need to be one?
You do NOT need to be an accredited investor to join. Every member can watch founder pitch meetings and read deal memos. It's a great way to learn, even if you're not yet accredited. However, you will have to certify that you are accredited before you are eligible to make an investment in one of our deals.
Could I lose money?
Yes, startups are risky and you can lose money you invest. This is why we research every deal, members spread their own checks, and no one is ever required to make an investment. The membership dues are not an investment and purchase a year of classes and community gatherings.
When and where do you meet?
We gather September through May and rest in the summer. In-person gatherings happen along the Wasatch Front, and the Pitch Parties are online. Calendar events are published in advance, and if you join mid-season you get access to everything available online.
How does joining work?
Applying is free. Tell us about yourself, book a conversation with Tori, and decide together whether membership is right for you. Nothing is final before that conversation.
Who can hold my second seat?
Your mother or your daughter(s) are invited to join because we want more families learning to talk with each other about money. (It is not as taboo as grandma said!) The second seat is $500 a year for Founding Members, and included for Community Stewards. Everyone has access to a full membership and all the benefits. Please note she will have to consent and complete her own application.
