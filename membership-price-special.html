Five ways to make the founding rate feel like a privilege.

The previous set all said discount. These say granted. The vocabulary shifts from retail (strike, save, percent off) to ceremony: a plate, a seal, a held rate, a ledger with her line on it.

All five use gold rather than lime. Gold is the rarest colour in your palette and already carries recognition on the Steward card, which is exactly the register a founding rate wants.

Why gold and not red. Red says clearance. Gold says first, few, and named. Your brand book reserves gold for recognition, and the founding rate is recognition with a number attached. If the concern is that gold belongs to Stewards, the honest answer is that the two never appear on the same card.
