Dear Utah women,

Last year, women here started strong companies and raised real money. I watched the checks arrive from California and New York, because investors here passed. And in rooms across our state, I kept hearing the same tired line: Utah women don't start investable companies.

The companies were real. The money was real. Only the belief was wrong.

So I stopped arguing with it. In January, I am gathering the women who will screen the companies, learn the craft, and write the checks ourselves. Everyone can be a member: for free, for $1,000, or for $5,000, depending on the part you want to play.

There has never been a better time to be early.

Tori Horton
Founder, Silver & Salt Capital
