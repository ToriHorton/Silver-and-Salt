The details, when you want them.

The classes and workshops
Six classes on money and values, developed by Invest for Better, the national program that teaches women to invest with their values, and led by Tori Horton and Lauren Sercu. Four in-person workshops a year with national money experts. The tools and templates are yours on day one. Other programs charge $2,000 a year for classes like these.
The gatherings
We gather September through June and rest in July and August: a drop-in hour every month, four online Pitch Parties, four in-person workshops along the Wasatch Front, your six classes, and a weekend retreat once a year (tickets separate, members pay 20 percent less). You get every date the day you join.
The investment opportunities
Each year we find and screen six to ten Utah companies founded by women, with 40 or more hours of research on every deal we finish. Every member watches every pitch and reads every deal memo. Investing itself is a separate decision, deal by deal, open to accredited investors, and money moves only when you say yes. Investments with that much research behind them earned 7.1 times the money (Wiltbank and Boeker).
The receipt
In 2025, half a cent of every venture dollar in Utah reached a woman-founded team (0.5 percent, per our Utah Funding 2025 analysis). Every year we publish what we paid to bring in experts, what capital reached women-founded companies, how many women learned to invest or wrote a first check, and what we gave back. Our first gift is set: $2,500 to Mama High-Five Grants in 2026.
Common questions
Do I have to invest? No. Membership is complete on its own, and many members join to learn and be part of the community.

Am I an "accredited investor"? That is a federal definition (income over $200,000 a year, or $1 million outside your home) and it only matters if you choose to write a check. It is never part of the application.

Could I lose money? Dues buy a year of classes and community, full stop. Investing in a startup is a separate choice and carries real risk, which is why we research hard and never require it.

How does joining work? Applying is free. Tell us about yourself, book a conversation with Tori, and decide together.
