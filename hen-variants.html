The Silver & Salt Capital Hen

Five different hens this time. Same family, same palette, five characters: pick who she is.

1 · The Softie

Round and warm, wing raised in welcome. The gentlest version of her.

2 · The Boss

Bandana on, wings on hips. The one who organizes the whole barnyard.

3 · The Elegant

Tall and composed, wheat held like a long-stemmed rose. The investor.

4 · The Broody Mama

Settled in low, keeping the loaf warm under her wing. Nobody leaves hungry.

5 · The Chick

Toddler proportions, one warm roll. Where every member starts.
