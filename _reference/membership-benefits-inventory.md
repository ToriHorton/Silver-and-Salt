# Membership benefits: the full inventory

**Status:** holding doc, 2026-08-03. Nothing here is decided or live.
**Purpose:** one place for every benefit that exists, so the cards can be built
from the whole list rather than from whatever a previous mockup happened to carry.

Sources: `membership-offerings-complete-brainstorm.md` (Drive, Jul 13/17),
`membership-website-copy-2026-07-24.md` (Drive), the live `membership.html`
before it was reverted, the G6 card mockup, and this session's edits.

**Column key.** ON CARD = appears on a membership card today.
PARKED = written and approved somewhere, currently promised nowhere.

-----

## 1. What the cards say today

### Community Steward, $5,000 a year, no seat limit
*For anyone who wants this shift to be sustainable*

1. Everything in Founding Member
2. 2 hours of complimentary and private financial coaching
3. A private hour with every visiting expert, before the workshop
4. The annual retreat, included
5. Your name recognized as a Community Steward

Plus a thank-you note (collapsed on phones): Community Stewards fund the
gatherings, the team, and the wealth experts.

### Founding Member, $1,000 a year
*Designed for women in Utah*

1. 6 money classes, developed with Invest for Better
2. More than 20 optional gatherings a year, including workshops with wealth experts
3. Live pitch meetings, deal memos, and the option to invest\*
4. A voice in finding and selecting the women we fund
5. A second membership included, for your mother or your daughter

### Associate Member, free
*Open to everyone, anywhere*

1. Every founder pitch, recorded and posted
2. The full deal memo on every company we finish
3. The option to invest, once accredited\*
4. The monthly update

Plus (collapsed on phones): what is held for members, and two objections answered.

## 4. What this surfaces

**Roughly 30 of the 40 items appear on no card.** Some belong nowhere near a
card. But several are concrete, high-value, and currently invisible: the member
resource library (4), the Impact Term Sheet ready on day one (5), the recorded
library (6), the stress-test model (7), the published annual calendar (12), the
annual portfolio statement (21), and the member directory with brokered
introductions (24, 25).

**The brainstorm's own top-value claims are the ones missing.** It names item 23,
the room of women who talk about money out loud, as "the number one draw in every
founding member conversation," and item 15, joining the diligence, as "the single
most valuable education in angel investing." Neither is a bullet on any card.

**Two items still need a number before they can be promised:** the support floor
(33) and the mothers-and-daughters price (27, now folded in as an included seat).
