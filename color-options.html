Replacing the green

The lime is fighting the plum and gold. Here are five replacements, each shown on both places it appears: the "Can I actually do this" card, and the "deployed by you / working for you" end of the pipeline.

Every colour is already in your palette, so nothing here introduces a new hue to the brand.

1 · Gold ■

The page's own accent. Most cohesive, and the risk is that gold already carries the icons, the labels and the founding band, so it stops meaning anything in particular.

My pick is teal. It is the complement to plum, which is why it reads as a deliberate second colour instead of an accident, and it still signals go at the end of the pipeline. Gold cannot do that job on this page because gold is already the page's everything: icons, labels, the founding band.

Moss is the safe answer if you would rather the card recede and let the copy carry it. Plum-pop is the most elegant and the least legible as a signal. Copper is lovely but sits too close to gold.
