Add Silver & Salt Capital to your LinkedIn

Your LinkedIn tells your network who you are and what you back. Adding Silver & Salt Capital builds your credibility as an investor, brings founders and deal flow to our community, and makes us visible to the founders and investors we want to find us. Which membership do you hold?

Every suggestion on this page updates to match, so you are never pasting the wrong one.

Founding Members hold the founding rate for as long as they stay.

Step One

Update your headline.

Your headline is the first thing anyone sees on LinkedIn. Paste this after your current title so your membership reads at a glance.

Suggested headline
Replace [Your title] with your actual title
[Your title] | Founding Member, Silver & Salt Capital
Step Two

Choose how to add your involvement.

Two standard placements. Pick whichever fits your profile narrative. Experience reads more professional; Volunteer Experience reads more community driven. Either works.

Option A Recommended
Add as Experience

Treats your membership like a professional role. Appears in your main Experience timeline.

  1. Go to your LinkedIn profile. In the Experience section, click the + button, then Add position.
  2. Fill in the fields using the suggestions below.
  3. Paste one of the description templates and save.
Suggested Fields
TitleFounding Member
CompanySilver & Salt Capital
TypeRemote
LocationYour city, Utah (or "Remote")
Start dateThe month you joined
Option B
Add as a Volunteer

Treats your membership as community involvement. Appears in a separate section further down your profile.

  1. On your profile, click Add profile section.
  2. Under Additional, choose Add volunteer experience.
  3. Fill in the fields, paste a description, and save.
Suggested Fields
OrganizationSilver & Salt Capital
RoleFounding Member
CauseEconomic Empowerment
Start dateThe month you joined
Step Three

Copy a description.

Three on-brand options. Pick the one that matches where you're placing Silver & Salt Capital on your profile, then tap Copy and paste into the Description field.

Founding Member: Experience
For the Experience section (recommended)
Silver & Salt Capital is an angel investment community for women in Utah. As a founding member, I help research and select the Utah companies we back, and I invest in the ones I believe in. Members back founders deal by deal, with a minimum of $1,000, with the intent of building a diversified portfolio of more than 30 companies over five years. We take on a company only after real diligence, and every decision is the member's own. There is no fund and no pooled commitment. Deals are structured as SPVs on a syndicate platform, so individual checks become a single, clean line on the founder's cap table. The community also runs a full year of investor education, so women who are new to this can learn it properly rather than guessing.
Founding Member: Volunteer
For the Volunteer section
Silver & Salt Capital is an angel investment community for women in Utah. As a founding member, I help research the companies we back and individually fund the Utah founders I believe in, deal by deal, starting at $1,000. Our thesis: companies founded by women deliver stronger returns per dollar invested, yet rarely see capital. We're building the infrastructure to change that, connecting capital to the Utah founders who use it best, one founder at a time.
