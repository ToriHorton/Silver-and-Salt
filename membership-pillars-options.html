Option 1 · Three pillars, literally

Three equal columns, one per pillar. The most scannable at a glance and the strongest visual echo of "3 pillars." Value notes live inside each card, so nothing needs reading order.

Your membership benefits, in 3 pillars.

Pick and choose what matters to you.

1

Education about money

Six weeks of training, plus workshops and office hours all year.

  • The Invest for Better course: six weeks in a cohort of about 20 women, from thoughtfully picking your bank to bonds and private equity, all through the lens of your values. Developed by Janine Firpo, led by impact investment advisor Lauren Sercu, and facilitated by Tori Horton.
  • Quarterly evening workshops with national money experts.
  • A monthly office hour with Tori and the community.
Other programs charge $2,000 a year for classes like these. The classes alone are worth more than the membership price.
2

Deals & due diligence

Learn by doing, on real Utah deals.

  • Four pitch gatherings a year: founders pitch live, every member is invited into the due diligence.
  • Real analysis, together: financials, business plan, marketing strategy, market size, deal terms.
  • Roughly 40 hours of research on every deal, with you on the inside.
  • You invest deal by deal; money moves only when you say yes.
If you've been thinking about starting or joining a fund, this is for you.
3

Friends & connections

Some women join for this alone.

  • We show up: with capital, with knowledge, with relationships, with visibility, with advocacy.
  • We lead with curiosity and assume positive intent.
  • We are kind and we are effective. Both, always.
  • We amplify each other's voices and operate from abundance, not scarcity.
Every membership holds two seats. Your mother or your daughter gets a seat beside you, included.
