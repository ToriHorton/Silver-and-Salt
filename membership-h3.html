A dispatch from the future we intend to print

Utah women rewrote who gets funded

For years, the state told itself a story: Utah women don't start investable companies. The 2025 numbers told a different one. Women here founded strong companies and raised real money, but the checks came from California and New York, because Utah investors passed (Utah Funding 2025).

In 2026, a group of women named the actual problem: Utah did not need convincing founders. It needed investors who fund women. In 2027, they stopped asking and organized: classes, research, gatherings, and a membership open to everyone who wanted the change.

The founding hundred learned to invest together, screened Utah companies founded by women, and wrote checks with their own hands. Asked what changed, one member said it plainly: nobody saw us as investors, so we became them.
