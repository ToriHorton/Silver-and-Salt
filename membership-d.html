Path One · Membership

Learn to invest, with women beside you.

Most of us were never taught how money grows, or how to build wealth. We were taught to save, to stretch a penny, to make do. It's time to make our dollars compound.

Membership is a year of learning how, together: classes that start from the very beginning, workshops with national experts we bring to Utah, and gatherings all year long. And because our community looks closely at real Utah companies founded by women, you learn on the real thing, with a front-row seat to every company we consider.

  • Classes in plain English, built for women starting from zero
  • More than 20 gatherings a year, in person and online
  • A second seat, included, for your mother or your daughter
  • Every company presentation and research report, even if you never invest
See membership details →

Open to women in Utah. No investing experience needed, and you never need to be an accredited investor to belong.

Path Two · Investing Together

Back women-founded companies, together.

Investing alone is hard. You have to find the companies, judge them by yourself, and write a big check on your own conviction. Investing as a group changes all of it: we find the companies together, research each one for weeks, and pool our checks so that even a smaller check helps fund something real. Then each investor decides for herself, company by company. Nobody ever invests your money but you.

  • Companies come to us: Utah founders present, and we dig in as a group
  • Weeks of shared research on every company before anyone writes a check
  • Pooled checks, so yours joins the others and goes further
  • Your decision, every time: money moves only when you say yes
Introduce Yourself →

Federal rules decide who can invest in private companies, based on income or savings. If that is you, or you are not sure, it starts with a conversation. You do not have to be a member to invest, and members never have to invest.

Path Three · The Movement

Watch Utah change who gets funded.

In 2025, half a cent of every venture dollar in Utah reached a woman-founded team: 0.5 percent. These women are changing that number, and we show our work: every year we publish the receipt, a plain report of what women here learned, funded, and built. Follow along free, one email a month, and watch it happen.
