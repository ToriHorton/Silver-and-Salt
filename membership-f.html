Last year, Utah investors wrote $1.86 billion in checks to 169 young companies. Follow that money with us for a moment.

Out of every dollar, half a cent reached a company founded by a woman. Not half a percent of the companies. Half a cent of the money.

Now the part that should make any investor sit up: women founders return 78 cents of revenue for every dollar invested. All-male teams return 31.

Better returns, and almost none of the funding. A market calls that an inefficiency. We call it our opening.
